Privacy Policy

This notice explains, in plain language, what personal data we process, why, and what you can do about it. It follows the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and the Information Technology Act 2000. We are the data fiduciary for the data described here.

What we collect and why

  • Account: your email address (to sign you in with a one-time code) and whether you are a brother or a sister (so introductions and Discussion Rooms can be paired correctly).
  • Profile: the details you choose to add, such as age, city, country, profession, education, languages, marriage goals and religious practice. Purpose: to show your profile to guardian-verified members of the opposite gender and to match introductions.
  • Guardian details: the name, relationship and email of the guardian you invite. Purpose: guardian approval, which is central to how Salaamify works.
  • Verification documents: identity and photo documents you upload. Purpose: to verify you. Only our authorised staff can see them, and they are deleted from storage as soon as we decide. We keep only the result.
  • Discussion Room messages: messages exchanged in guardian-supervised rooms, and our automatic checks for shared contact details. Purpose: to run the room, keep everyone safe and enforce our rules.
  • Payments: your name, email, state or country, optional GSTIN and payment references. Card, UPI and PayPal details are handled by Razorpay and PayPal; we never see or store them. Purpose: to provide your membership and issue tax invoices.
  • Support messages you send us, so we can reply.
  • Security data: IP address and request counts, kept briefly to stop abuse, and an audit log of administrative actions.

Consent and legal basis

We process your data with your consent, given when you sign in and create a profile, and where the law permits for legitimate uses such as tax records, preventing fraud and responding to legal requests. You can withdraw consent at any time by closing your account.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data and we do not show advertising. We use these service providers to run the site: Cloudflare (hosting, database and file storage), Resend (email), Razorpay (payments in India) and PayPal (payments from outside India). They process data only for us. Your profile is visible only to other signed-in members under our rules, and your guardians see what is needed to approve introductions. Some providers store data outside India. We may disclose data if the law or a competent authority requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Verification documents: deleted when we decide.
  • Profile, guardians, verification results and sign-in data: until you close your account, then erased.
  • Your messages in rooms: replaced with "[removed]" when you close your account.
  • Tax invoices and payment records: kept for the period tax law requires.
  • Security logs: kept briefly, and audit logs for as long as needed for safety and accountability.

Your rights

You may access, correct and erase your data, withdraw consent, nominate someone to exercise your rights on your behalf if you die or cannot act, and complain to us. Signed-in members can download their data or close their account from the dashboard (section "Your data"). Otherwise write to peace@pioushalf.com from your registered email. We acknowledge within 24 hours and aim to resolve within 15 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operating.

Children

Salaamify is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly process children's data. If a profile appears to belong to someone under 18, it is removed.

Security and breaches

We use encrypted connections, restricted staff access, rate limits, an audit log and automatic error monitoring. If a breach affecting your data occurs, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Cookies

We use one essential sign-in cookie. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Changes

If we change this notice in a material way we will update the date above and tell members by email.
