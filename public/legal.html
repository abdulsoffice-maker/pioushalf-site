Privacy Policy

We collect name, email, mobile number, state, optional GSTIN and payment references so we can provide memberships and issue tax invoices. Data is stored in Cloudflare D1 and R2. Card and UPI details are handled only by Razorpay. We do not sell personal data. You may ask to access, correct or erase your data, or withdraw consent, by emailing peace@pioushalf.com; we respond within 30 days. Tax records are retained as long as the law requires.

Terms of Use

Salaamify™ is a guardian-led introduction service for marriage only. Members must be adults, provide truthful information and follow the Community Guidelines. Sharing contact details before both guardians approve, harassment or misuse leads to suspension. Membership starts on payment confirmation and ends at the end of the chosen period.

Refund Policy

Eligible sisters receive a 50% discount after verification; any difference from the amount charged is refunded to the original payment method. If you ask for cancellation within 48 hours of payment and have not sent or received any interest, we refund the fee in full. After that, fees are non-refundable except where the law requires. Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 working days.

Disclaimer

Salaamify™ provides introductions only. We do not guarantee marriage, and families should do their own due diligence.

Community Guidelines

Keep conversation respectful and marriage-focused. Do not share phone numbers, social handles, emails or addresses before approval. Do not pressure, deceive or harass anyone.
