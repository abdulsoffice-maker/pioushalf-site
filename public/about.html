Marriage, the proper way

Salaamify™ helps practising Muslims find a righteous spouse through a safe, family-centred and Shariah-conscious process. It is not a dating site.

No dating. No hidden conversations. No unsupervised messaging. Only purposeful introductions for marriage.

What makes us different

Guardians lead

Every member adds a guardian (wali or parent). Introductions begin only after the guardians approve.

No private chat

There are no inboxes. Conversation happens in a Marriage Discussion Room with a guardian present and a marriage-focused purpose.

Contact details stay protected

Phone numbers, social handles, emails and addresses are blocked automatically until both guardians approve.

Verified members

Email, identity, photo and guardian checks build a visible trust score. Identity documents are deleted once we decide.

Clarity before commitment

A Marriage Safety and Alignment step lets families confirm important matters in writing. These are recommendations that help you talk openly, and the sister always has the final say.

Privacy first

We never sell data and we show no advertising. You can download your data or close your account at any time.

Why “PiousHalf”?

The Prophet ﷺ taught that when a person marries, he has completed half of the religion, and should fear Allah regarding the other half. PiousHalf is the righteous partner who helps you complete it.

Common questions

No. All conversation happens in guardian-supervised Marriage Discussion Rooms.
